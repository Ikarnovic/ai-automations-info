Personal automation platform

Schedules kept in sync.

AI Automations helps its owner synchronize work shifts, sports schedules, and exam information with selected Google calendars and spreadsheets.

What the app does

  • Creates and updates calendar events from configured work and sports schedules, and removes obsolete synchronized events when required.
  • Reads course and exam information from a configured Google spreadsheet and writes updated exam tables.
  • Records automation results in a local dashboard and sends configured change and failure notifications through Discord.

Why Google access is needed

Google Calendar access enables event synchronization. Google Sheets access enables reading configured course information and updating exam information. The owner authorizes access through Google; this informational website does not request Google access.

Personal use

The automation services run in the owner's local environment. This website provides information about the app and its data practices; it does not provide access to the dashboard or automation controls.

Read the privacy policy for details about access, storage, sharing, and removal of data.
