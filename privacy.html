Data practices

Privacy policy

AI Automations is a personal automation platform operated by its owner. This policy describes the configured calendar, spreadsheet, notification, and local assistance features.

Last updated: September 30, 2026.

Data accessed and its purpose

The app uses Google OAuth authorization to access configured Google calendars and spreadsheets. Calendar data may include calendar and event identifiers, titles, descriptions, dates, times, and locations. Spreadsheet data includes configured course identifiers, exam dates, and existing exam-table values.

These data are used to synchronize work and sports events, preserve existing synchronization references, and update exam tables. The app also processes schedule information from its configured source services.

Storage and retention

OAuth credentials are stored by the local n8n service. The local database stores synchronization records, Google event identifiers, automation history, errors, and related metadata. Workflow execution records and backups can also contain data processed during runs.

Records are retained until the owner removes them; there is no general automatic deletion period. Disabling an automation stops its executions but preserves existing records and Google events. Backups may retain older copies until the owner removes those backups.

Sharing and notifications

Google receives the calendar and spreadsheet requests needed for synchronization. Configured Discord notifications can include schedule changes and diagnostic error details. Anyone with access to those Discord channels may see those messages. Relevant service providers process requests under their own policies.

The app does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising. This public informational website contains no automation records or OAuth credentials.

Optional local AI assistance

When the owner uses the local assistant, selected automation metadata, recent run summaries and errors, sanitized workflow details, and any supplied messages or screenshots may be processed by a locally hosted model. Conversation text and proposals are saved in the local database. The feature is used for on-demand assistance, not to train a general-purpose model.

Access and control

The owner can revoke the app's Google authorization in Google Account connections and can disable individual automations in the local dashboard. Revocation prevents future authorized Google requests but does not remove existing local records, backups, calendar events, or spreadsheet content. Those copies must be removed separately by their owner.

Protection of data

The automation services and stored records are maintained in the owner's environment. Access depends on the security of that environment and the accounts and channels the owner configures. Google credentials are not included in this website.

Google API data use

AI Automations' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Contact and changes

For questions or data-removal requests, contact the operator using the user support email shown on the app's Google consent screen. This policy should be updated when the app's data practices change.
